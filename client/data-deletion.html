Data Deletion Instructions

How to request removal of eligible information associated with feedback authentication, feedback submissions, or contact messages.

JPano PortfolioEffective October 1, 2026

Your deletion option

You may request deletion of eligible personal information associated with feedback authentication, submitted feedback, a live-chat conversation, or a contact request. This page provides the public deletion instructions used by JPano Portfolio and its connected authentication providers.

How to request deletion

Open the Contact section and send a message with the subject Data deletion request. State which information you want removed and, when applicable, identify the sign-in provider you used for feedback.

Information that helps locate your record

You may include the name shown on your feedback and the email, username, or provider account reference associated with the submission. Do not send your password, OAuth token, authentication code, or any other account secret.

What may be deleted

  • Stored feedback identity information associated with the request.
  • Feedback content associated with that identity when applicable.
  • Eligible contact information submitted through the portfolio contact form.
  • Live-chat messages and associated short-lived moderation metadata. Chat data is also automatically removed after 7 days.

Verification of a request

For safety, additional information may be requested when necessary to reasonably verify that a deletion request relates to the requester. Requests are limited to information controlled by JPano Portfolio.

Third-party accounts

Deleting information from JPano Portfolio does not delete your Google, GitHub, Facebook, Telegram, or other third-party account. Account deletion with those providers must be handled directly through the provider.

Operational records

Some minimal records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or integrity of administrative records. Where possible, retained records are limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Completion

After the request has been reviewed and the applicable information has been located, eligible records will be removed from the active portfolio data. If more information is needed to complete the request, a reply may be sent using the contact details you provided.

Need help?

If you cannot use the normal contact flow, return to JPano Portfolio and use any available direct contact option shown in the Contact section.
