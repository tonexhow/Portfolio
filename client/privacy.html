Privacy Policy

How JPano Portfolio collects, uses, protects, and removes information when you interact with the website and its feedback verification features.

JPano PortfolioEffective October 1, 2026

What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how JPano Portfolio handles information submitted through the portfolio website, including feedback verification, public feedback, live chat, contact messages, and basic visit records.

Information you provide

  • Contact form details such as your name, email address, subject, and message.
  • Feedback rating and comment when you submit feedback.
  • Account identity details returned by a supported sign-in provider when you choose to verify feedback.

Social sign-in information

For verified feedback, a provider such as Google, GitHub, Facebook, or Telegram may return a display name, provider-specific account identifier, email or username when available, and profile image. The site uses this information only to associate an identity with submitted feedback and to support moderation.

Public display and masking

Approved feedback may be displayed publicly. Identifying text such as names and account credentials is masked on the public portfolio. Administrators may view the stored identity details when needed for moderation and account verification.

Technical information

The site may create limited operational records needed for security, rate limiting, administration, and visit analytics. Live chat may temporarily record the server-observed IP address, browser/user-agent, platform, Cloudflare country code when available, referrer, and a short IP-plus-user-agent fingerprint so the administrator can review spam or abuse. The site does not use canvas, audio, or hardware fingerprinting for chat.

Live chat

Live chat is text-only and is available only while an authenticated administrator is actively connected. Chat messages and their moderation metadata are automatically removed after 7 days. If you already signed in through a supported OAuth flow, the chat may associate that authorized display name and profile image with the conversation; otherwise the chat remains anonymous.

How information is used

  • To deliver and moderate portfolio feedback.
  • To respond to contact messages.
  • To protect the website from abuse and repeated automated submissions.
  • To maintain basic site administration and usage records.

Third-party providers

If you choose a third-party sign-in provider, that provider processes the login according to its own terms and privacy practices. JPano Portfolio does not store the provider access token used during the OAuth sign-in process.

Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while it is reasonably needed for moderation, communication, security, or site records. You may request deletion of feedback-related identity information and other eligible submitted data by following the Data Deletion Instructions.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact section of JPano Portfolio. Include enough information for the request to be identified without sending passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.
